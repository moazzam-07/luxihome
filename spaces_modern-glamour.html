MODERN GLAMOUR

House of Honey in West Hollywood: Modern Glamour

A Creative Haven in Blush & Brass
In the middle of West Hollywood is a three-level home with a penchant for glamour.
We reimagined it for a young New York transplant whose world moves between fashion, beauty, and art. The interiors lean into a flirty, high-impact palette, layered with texture, shine, and just enough attitude. There are standout moments throughout, including a neon-lit entry and a rooftop that tends to steal the scene.
It feels polished, playful, and ready for whatever the night brings.
Yoshihiro Makino Photography
