About Square M Design

Square M Design is a British chartered landscape design consultancy specialising in high-end residential, hospitality and resort developments.

LUXiHOME is a special and unique place, so how you reach each villa should be special too. We collaborated closely with the architects and designers to build a uniquely detailed connection between each indoor-outdoor space. Carefully curated outdoor wellness, social, active and dining elements are positioned in calming layers of landscape, cascading pools and timeless terraces percolating down to your private beach.

Quintin Davidson, Founder / CEO
