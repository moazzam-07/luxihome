NEIGHBORHOOD GUIDES • KOLKATA

South Kolkata 3BHK Flat Makeovers: Gariahat, Jodhpur Park & Lake Gardens

Apartments built in the late 1990s and early 2000s across Gariahat, Southern Avenue, Jodhpur Park, and Lake Gardens boast generous carpet footprints and prime connectivity, but their interiors feel dated, dark, and acoustically compromised.

Key Takeaways
  • •Upgrade obsolete 2.5 sq.mm aluminum/copper wiring to fire-resistant low-smoke (FRLS) 4 sq.mm electrical cabling for modern appliances.
  • •Convert redundant dark servant rooms into coveted walk-in dressers or dedicated work-from-home soundproof pods.
  • •Replace noisy, water-leaking aluminum sliding windows with acoustic UPVC double-glazed systems.
  • •Reconfigure cramped, dark compartmentalized kitchens into bright, breakfast-counter open social layouts.

1. Upgrading Outdated Electrical & Plumbing Infrastructure

Older South Kolkata buildings were wired for a maximum 3kW load—sufficient for a refrigerator and ceiling fans. Today’s modern lifestyles demand 10kW to 15kW: multiple inverter ACs, dishwashers, induction cooktops, and microwave ovens.

Our overhaul begins by stripping out old concealed iron conduits that have rusted, installing new distribution boards (DB) with dedicated MCBs for every room, and upgrading the mainline feeder cable.

2. En-Suite Bathroom Expansions

Bathrooms in 2000s flats were cramped 4x6 utility boxes. By taking 18 inches from an oversized adjacent corridor, we install frameless glass shower enclosures, wall-hung concealed cisterns, and niche ambient vanity lighting.

Insider Trade Tip

The Housing Society Debris & Water Ingress Rule

In established South Kolkata apartment associations, debris disposal down common stairwells is strictly regulated and will incur fines. Always mandate that your contractor uses heavy-duty canvas debris bags and books pre-authorized municipal removal trucks. Never dump debris on public pavements.
