Ranch Redux

House of Honey in Montecito: Ranch Redux

Strong Bones, Better Instincts
Set in Hope Ranch, this classic California ranch had strong bones and an easy connection to the outdoors.
We approached it with restraint, keeping the architecture largely intact while refining how the home moves. Sightlines were opened, transitions softened, and the interiors given a more fluid, intuitive rhythm. The palette leans warm and tactile, with a few deliberate contrasts to keep it from feeling too settled.
It now feels open, expressive, and entirely at ease with itself.
Mellon Studio Photography
