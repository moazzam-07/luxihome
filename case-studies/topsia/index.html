Topsia • Atmosphere Corridor

The Sky Villa at EM Bypass

Perched 30 floors above the EM Bypass in Topsia, this 5,900 sq.ft sky duplex offers breathtaking views across Kolkata and the East Wetlands. LUXiHOME designed every corner to match the building’s curved architecture, delivering a weightless, hotel-style living experience.

Area 5,900 Sq.Ft
Typology High-Rise Sky Duplex
Location EM Bypass / Topsia
Scope Bespoke Space Transformation
Materials Portoro Marble, Bouclé & Venetian Plaster
Timeline 12 Months Delivery
THE FULL PROJECT BRIEF

The Challenge & How We Solved It

THE CLIENT'S PROBLEM

What Needed Fixing

The apartment’s curved exterior walls made standard rectangular furniture look awkward, creating wasted dead corners. Harsh western sun made the upper floor very hot in the late afternoon, and the raw high-ceiling living room felt hollow and impersonal.

THE LUXIHOME SOLUTION

How We Delivered

We custom-designed curved banquettes, curved marble credenzas, and soft bouclé seating that hug the glass perimeter seamlessly. Motorized thermal solar blinds lower automatically during peak sunlight hours. Soft Venetian plaster walls and warm curved cove lighting turned the tall space into an intimate, luxurious retreat.
