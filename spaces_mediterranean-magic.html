MEDITERRANEAN MAGIC

House of Honey in Solana Beach: Mediterranean Magic

Spanish Roots, California Heart
Located in Solana Beach, this sunny Mediterranean home began as a blank slate.
We led a full renovation, shaping it into an elegant and airy home for a young family building their life in real time. The approach balances the home’s Spanish roots with a more relaxed, contemporary sensibility, using light, pattern, and tactile materials to support both everyday living and gathering.
It now feels inviting, expressive, and built to grow with them.
Manolo Langis Photography
