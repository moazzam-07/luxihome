Interactive "Deep-Zoom" Scrollytelling Concept

This photo is not a standard static team picture. On the live website, the section will lock in place as the user scrolls. The camera will dynamically zoom in (300% to 400%) onto the Owner's face with animated bio details, then pan smoothly across the luxury cafe/restaurant to each of the other 4 leadership members sequentially. The technical specs below guarantee zero pixelation, no edge-clipping, and immaculate luxury editorial quality.

Priority Checklist (Ranked by Critical Impact)
Priority #1 ★★★★★ MOST CRITICAL
High-Resolution Camera Body (45MP–60MP+)
Shoot in RAW on a professional full-frame camera (Sony A7R IV/V, Canon R5, Nikon Z8/Z9). Zero smartphones.
Why: The website code crops/zooms up to 400% into individual faces on 4K retina displays. A smartphone photo will turn into blurry pixelated noise. High-res RAW ensures crisp eyelashes and tailored suit fabrics.
Priority #2 ★★★★★ CRITICAL
25% Environmental "Breathing Room"
Frame the shot wide. Leave at least 20% to 25% empty background around the top, bottom, left, and right.
Why: When the camera zooms into the person on the far left or far right, if their body touches the photo edge, the viewport runs out of picture and reveals empty black space. Generous padding allows infinite smooth pan & zoom.
Priority #3 ★★★★ HIGH
Universal Sharp Focus (f/5.6 – f/8)
Stop down aperture to f/5.6 or f/8. Shutter speed at 1/250s or faster on a heavy tripod. Zero motion blur.
Why: Photographers love shallow bokeh (f/1.8), but with 5 people, shallow focus blurs people on the sides or slightly behind. Every single face must be tack-sharp when zoomed in.
Priority #4 ★★★★ HIGH
Staggered Luxury Spacing (No Clumping)
Arrange the 5 people in a layered, natural composition (e.g. 2–3 seated at a marble table/lounge, 2 standing naturally).
Why: Avoid flat straight-line mugshots. Crucially, leave breathing room between heads so when the camera zooms into one person, their text bio card has room to slide in without covering another face.
Priority #5 ★★★ MEDIUM
16:9 Landscape Aspect Ratio
Shoot horizontally in 16:9 (or 3:2 full sensor). Keep camera at eye-level to seated subjects.
Why: 16:9 widescreen naturally matches modern desktop viewports. On mobile screens (9:16), our code dynamically centers and crops onto the active zoomed subject.
Priority #6 ★★ AESTHETIC
Warm Architectural Lighting & Wardrobe
Warm ambient cafe glow (2700K–3000K). Coordinated luxury palette: Deep Navy, Charcoal, Black, Cream, Beige.
Why: Avoid harsh direct overhead ceiling spotlights that cast dark shadows under eyes. Neutral tailored luxury tones seamlessly match LUXiHOME's pure white and navy brand aesthetic.
