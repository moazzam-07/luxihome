A Roaming Heart: The Morris Sofa, Wanderlust Edition

A Roaming Heart: The Morris Sofa, Wanderlust Edition

We've always believed a sofa should do more than politely sit in a room. It should flirt. Command attention. Maybe even start a little trouble. Enter the Morris Sofa by Lulu Lafortune - a design crush of ours since 2018 with its wide seat, swooping curves, and unapologetic confidence.
We collaborated with visionary artist Meg Jorgenson to create our own custom version, the Wanderlust Edition. Think bold paint strokes, a little attitude, and just enough irreverence to make you wonder if you should really be sitting on it (spoiler: you should).
Naturally, we threw a cocktail party to celebrate. Guests sipped, sprawled, and watched as Jorgenson painted live - because if you're going to launch a sofa, why not turn it into performance art?
We also unveiled our latest window installation, "A Roaming Heart" - a love letter to wanderlust with a few House of Honey twists. Call it a passport in design form: playful, a bit surreal, and begging for adventure.
The Morris Sofa Wanderlust Edition is a statement, a dare, maybe even a conversation you didn't see coming.
