The Rajarhat Residence

Chinar Park / Action Area II, Rajarhat • Contemporary Urban Manor

Modernist Elegance & Tactile Grandeur in Rajarhat

Located in the flourishing cosmopolitan hub of Chinar Park and Action Area II, Rajarhat, this luxury residence embodies effortless modern grandeur. Every spatial volume was orchestrated by LUXiHOME to combine tactile intimacy with sweeping architectural open-concept flow.

From sculpted cream bouclé living lounges anchored by charcoal acoustic fluted walls and cursive neon wave chandeliers, to Calacatta marble dining salons, tufted suede master chambers, and minimalist white culinary suites—every nuance was crafted with uncompromising precision.

01 / Reception Salon

The Formal Living Salon

Sculptural cream bouclé armchairs and a faceted geometric stone cocktail table face a dark vertical slat acoustic media feature wall, complemented by an artistic luminous wave chandelier and open visual harmony with the dining suite.

• Sculptural Cream Bouclé • Fluted Charcoal Media Wall • Luminous Wave Chandelier

02 / Dining Salon

The Calacatta Dining Salon

A polished Calacatta marble dining table accompanied by curved two-tone leather and velvet dining chairs with brass-tipped tapered legs, abstract canvas art, and a suspended triple halo LED luminaire.

• Calacatta Marble Dining Table • Two-Tone Curved Velvet Chairs • Triple Halo LED Rings

03 / Private Chamber

Primary Master Suite

A soothing master retreat centered around a tufted grey suede upholstered bed, vertical charcoal acoustic panels, a mirrored dressing alcove, and a stepped geometric ceiling tray with warm perimeter backlighting.

• Tufted Suede Upholstery • Stepped Geometric Ceiling Tray • Charcoal Fluted Paneling

04 / Culinary Atelier

Modernist Kitchen Atelier

Pristine white lacquer cabinetry accented with sleek black bar hardware, twin recessed warm ceiling light troughs, speckled granite countertops, and an integrated appliance laundry station.

Calacatta Spa Ensuite

Full-height Calacatta white marble wall and floor tiling with dramatic grey veining, contrasting matte black overhead rainfall shower system, and minimalist ceramic vanity.
