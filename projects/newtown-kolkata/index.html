LUXiHOME Newtown

Bespoke visions of distinguished living in Newtown

At LUXiHOME (The Alam Interior ®), we transform luxury residences into deeply personal sanctuaries. Founded by Sj. Alam and Jammy Sheikh in Kolkata, our studio crafts turnkey interior architecture combining imported Italian marbles, tailored woodwork, and meticulous execution.

Each Newtown residence is an individual testament to craftsmanship, architectural precision, and enduring refinement. From monumental double-height living atriums to custom-milled fluted timber details, we shape spaces that define luxury.
