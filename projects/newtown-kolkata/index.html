Bespoke visions of distinguished living in Newtown

Imagine a home, not simply built but conceptualised entirely for you. LUXiHOME Newtown embodies the seamless convergence of avant-garde architecture, raw natural stone, and serene light-filled volumes rising in Kolkata's premier progressive district.
