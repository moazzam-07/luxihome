COOL RANCH

House of Honey on Mountain Drive: A Mid-Century Ranch

Modern Warmth in the Foothills
A Mid-Century ranch in Montecito that had drifted a bit from its roots.
We brought it back with a lighter hand, keeping the structure intact while introducing a more tactile, grounded palette. Wood, stone, and warmth replace anything extraneous, reconnecting the home to its setting. What remains is simpler, softer, and far more in tune with where it sits.
