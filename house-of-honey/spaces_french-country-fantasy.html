FRENCH COUNTRY FANTASY

House of Honey in Beverly Hills: French Country Fantasy

A Storybook Manor with Cinematic Soul
Tucked into a quiet cul-de-sac in Benedict Canyon, this French Country farmhouse arrived with beautiful bones and a slightly tangled point of view.
We were brought in to restore clarity without losing its sense of age and character. The architecture was left intact, while the interiors were layered with plaster, stone, and a mix of antique and contemporary pieces that feel collected rather than composed. There are moments of atmosphere throughout, including a trellised dining room with a hand-painted fresco and a moody red office that shifts with the light.
Now it reads as personal, layered, and comfortably lived in.
Michael Clifford Photography
