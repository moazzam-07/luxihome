A STUDY IN EXPRESSION

House of Honey in Pasadena: A Study in Expression

Color, Character, and the Art of Living Well
Just behind the Gamble House in Pasadena, this newly built residence had scale, presence, and plenty of ambition, but it needed a clearer point of view.
We approached it as a story in the making, creating a layered, color-driven interior for a couple bringing their lives together. Curves, bold tones, and a few irreverent moments shift the house away from the expected. There are delightful surprises throughout, including a scratch-and-sniff elevator that leaves an impression. It now feels personal, expressive, and fully its own.
Michael Clifford Photography
