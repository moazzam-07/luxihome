Good Taste, Bad Behavior

House of Honey in Montecito: Good Taste, Bad Behavior

Edge, Ease, and the Art of Not Playing It Safe
Some homes arrive fully formed. Others need a little provocation.
Set between the Montecito mountains and the ocean, this residence had all the hallmarks of its surroundings - Spanish influence, generous scale, an easy indoor-outdoor rhythm. But what it lacked was tension... a point of view. A reason to look twice.
That’s where we came in. Our clients - a young couple from the Bay Area with a sharp eye and a sense of humor - weren’t interested in playing it safe. They wanted a home that felt as dynamic as their lives: a place for hosting, for unwinding, for growing into.
The home reads as a series of thoughtful gestures - subtle shifts in material and form that build over time. Not a single statement, but a layered one - measured, personal, and intentionally open-ended.
