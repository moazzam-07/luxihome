The Salt Lake Manor

Sector I, Salt Lake • Luxury Private Estate

Bespoke visions of distinguished living

Nestled in the prestigious avenues of Sector I, Salt Lake, The Salt Lake Manor is an architectural testament to bespoke luxury and refined Indian royal grandeur. Every spatial volume was orchestrated by LUXiHOME to deliver an atmosphere of timeless sophistication.

From double-height floating staircases illuminated by cascading crystal chandeliers, to dark oak herringbone parquetry, smoked glass culinary joinery, and private open-to-sky rooftop lounges—every nuance was crafted with uncompromising precision.

01 / Foyer

Grand Atrium

A dramatic double-height volume welcoming guests with cantilevered solid walnut floating stair treads, seamless frameless glass balustrading, and a suspended spiral crystal butterfly chandelier creating magnificent light refractions throughout the atrium.

• Floating Walnut Cantilevers • Crystal Spiral Chandelier • Under-Tread Illumination
02 / Salon

Formal Living

Rich herringbone parquet in smoked European oak establishes a warm, timeless foundation for plush cream bespoke sectionals, complemented by modern brushed brass halo chandeliers and custom marble cocktail tables.

• Herringbone Parquetry • Brass Halo Chandelier • Velvet & Silk FF&E
03 / Atelier

Culinary Suite

A masterclass in contemporary culinary architecture. Floor-to-ceiling smoked fluted glass display vitrines with concealed vertical warm illumination, seamless Calacatta white quartz waterfall island, and bespoke dark walnut millwork.

• Smoked Fluted Glass • Calacatta Quartz Island • Integrated Warm LED
04 / Private Suite

Master Retreat

An intimate private sanctuary sculpted with symmetrical fluted acoustic timber wall paneling, tinted floor-to-ceiling bronze mirror reveals, custom reading armchairs, and automated ambient cove backlighting.

• Fluted Timber Walls • Bronze Mirror Insets • Custom Reading Lounge
05 / Wellness

Spa & Sky Deck

A master ensuite featuring monolithic black granite floating counters with dual vessel basins, walk-in glass shower room, and an effortless transition to the private rooftop sky terrace equipped with laser-cut geometric canopy shading.

• Black Granite Double Vanity • Frameless Rain Shower • Pergola Rooftop Sky Deck
